# PPH
Parkinsons App

PPH, a free to distribute and use mobile app, developed using MIT App Inventor fills a void in pre-clinical tremor measurement and PD patient care segment. 
PPH measures tremors and enhances the platform to serve as a PD information portal, provides self-care avenues, and mental and physical health activities by fostering patient engagement with a global community. 

The majority of competitors support PD patients by focusing on medications and medical information. 
While tremor measurement was seen in a very small percentage of apps, they did not measure individual axis (X, Y, Z) tremors or perform comprehensive tests which are vital for in-depth analysis. 

Our app’s main advantages are (a) providing a free product, (b) serving a wholesome experience, and (c) detailed tremor measurement.
